They buried my name beneath a chapel stone
Left me to winter with a kingdom overthrown
Seven candles trembled underneath a blood-red sky
But death forgot to teach this restless heart to die

I heard the old bells calling
From somewhere underground

CROWN OF ASH!
HEART OF THUNDER!
I rose where broken kings were buried under
Ring the bell and raise the flame
Let the ancient world remember my name
I crossed the dark with lightning in my hand

I watched the marble towers disappear in rain
Saw emperors buy glory with another soldier's pain
Every throne grows hungry, every empire digs a grave
And every crown eventually becomes another chain

I reached the sea when all my wars were done
No army followed, no kingdom had I won
The wind said, "Nothing living conquers time."
So I laid my rusted sword beside the tide

CROWN OF ASH!
BORN OF THUNDER!
No throne above me, no grave to pull me under
Break the sword and burn the throne
A thousand wars could never make me whole
I would rather walk this dying world alone

Let every monument fall...

I was never immortal.

I was only unafraid.
