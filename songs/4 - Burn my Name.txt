They found the crown beneath a dead king’s stone
Black as the night and cold as bone
Seven names were carved along the rim
Six were dead — The seventh one was mine

The priest said, “Leave it where it lies”
But gold has ways of making fools feel wise
I placed that circle on my head
And heard a thousand voices of the dead

One for blood
Two for flame
Three for every stolen name

Four for war
Five for pain
And six TO BRING THEM BACK AGAIN!

BLACK CROWN!
BURN MY NAME!

BLACK CROWN!
FEED THE FLAME!

I run — It follows!
I bleed — It knows!
Every road I take — THE SHADOW GROWS!

BLACK CROWN!
YOU WILL NOT TAKE MY SOUL!

I crossed the mountains under crimson moons
I slept in graves and empty rooms
Every village locked its doors
Before my shadow touched the floor

I fought a beast beside the northern wall
Watched seven blackened horsemen fall
But when the final rider died
He wore my face on the other side

One for steel
Two for breath
Three to bargain life with death

Four for kings
Five for lies
And six — TO CLOSE THE DEAD MAN’S EYES!

BLACK CROWN!
CALL MY NAME!

BLACK CROWN!
FEED THE FLAME!

I ride — It follows!
I strike — It knows!
Every enemy I kill — THE DARKNESS GROWS!

BLACK CROWN!
YOU WILL NOT TAKE CONTROL!

At last I reached the mountain
Where the first king lost his head

His throne was carved from iron
His hall was filled with dead

And on the wall behind him
One sentence burned in red:

“THE CROWN DOES NOT CURSE THE LIVING.”

“IT MAKES THE LIVING... JOIN THE DEAD.”

I tore the crown away and cast it down
The earth split open beneath the ground
A thousand voices screamed my name
But this time — I screamed back the same:

“You had your kings!
You had your wars!
You had your centuries behind locked doors!
You want another soul tonight?”

COME TAKE MINE!

BLACK CROWN!
HERE I STAND!

BLACK CROWN!
TAKE MY HAND!

No fear — No master!
No grave — No throne!
I crossed the world beneath your curse — BUT I DID NOT WALK ALONE!

BLACK CROWN!
YOUR REIGN IS DONE!
I BREAK THE CURSE—
BEFORE THE RISING SUN!

Six names fallen...
One remains...

Mine.
