Dearest Mother, I am writing from the road
My feet are sore, but I have dropped the heavy load
I run through the night, my heart broken in two
I had to leave to find a clearer sky of blue

The shadows of that house were choking out my breath
To stay behind was just a slow and silent death
My tears were falling as I fled into the night
With broken wings, I re-embraced my life and light

I have escaped the dark, I am finally free!
Though scars remain, the wind is carrying me!
I’ve crossed through the night, a sweet song on my cheek
And found the inner voice I was forbidden to speak!

Now as I hold this pen, my thoughts are all for you
I know the heavy chains that weigh upon your neck
Don't let the walls of fear keep holding you in place
There is a whole world beyond this dark embrace

Leave the shadow behind...
Follow my steps, run from the night...
Come to me, Mother... be free.
